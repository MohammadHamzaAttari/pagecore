## What Changed

The 2026 algorithm prioritizes three key signals above all else.

## The Three Signals

1. **Watch time** — Not just views, but how long people actually watch
2. **Shares** — Private shares (Messenger) weighted more than public shares
3. **Return rate** — How often viewers come back to your page

## Actionable Tips

- Aim for 70%+ retention in the first 30 seconds
- Create content people want to send to friends
- Post consistently to build return viewership
