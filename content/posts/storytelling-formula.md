## The Formula

Every one of our million-view videos follows this exact 4-part structure.

## Part 1: The Hook (0-3s)

Start with a bold statement, surprising fact, or provocative question.

## Part 2: The Build (3-30s)

Provide context and build tension. Why does this matter?

## Part 3: The Payoff (30-45s)

Deliver the main insight or revelation.

## Part 4: The CTA (45-60s)

Tell them what to do next — share, comment, follow.

## Case Study

Our highest-performing video used this exact structure and hit 2.3M views in one week.
