## Why Facebook Live?

Facebook Live videos get 6x more engagement than regular video posts. Here's how to make the most of them for your page.

## Preparation Tips

- Test your audio and video equipment at least 30 minutes before going live.
- Prepare talking points rather than a full script to keep things natural.
- Promote your live session 24 hours in advance across all your pages.

## During the Broadcast

Engage with comments in real-time, acknowledge viewers by name, and keep the energy high throughout. The interactive nature of live video is its biggest strength.

## Post-Processing

Save your live video and repurpose it as regular content. The replay often gets just as many views as the original broadcast.
