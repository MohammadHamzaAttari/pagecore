## The 3-Second Rule

If you don't hook them in 3 seconds, you've lost them. Here's how top editors do it.

## 5 Techniques

1. **Jump cuts** — Remove dead air aggressively
2. **Text overlays** — Highlight key words as you speak
3. **Sound effects** — A well-placed whoosh or ding adds energy
4. **Speed ramps** — Speed up slow parts, slow down key moments
5. **Color grading** — Consistent color palette builds brand recognition

## Practice

Pick two techniques and apply them to your next video.
